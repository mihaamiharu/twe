# AI-Assisted QA Workflow — Pilot Feedback Pack

## Final survey

Use a short survey after course completion. Ask learners to rate each item from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree), with an optional comment.

- I could complete the local setup without unnecessary friction.
- The fictional PRD gave me enough context to practice realistic QA work.
- The seven checkpoints formed a coherent workflow.
- The AI activities helped me think more clearly about QA work.
- The course made me verify and justify AI output rather than accept it blindly.
- I understand how to investigate a failing automation test.
- I feel more confident moving from Manual QA toward automation.
- The starter repository is useful as portfolio evidence.

## Open questions

- Which checkpoint was most valuable, and why?
- Which checkpoint was hardest, and why?
- Where did the setup or instructions become unclear?
- What did you expect to find but could not find?
- What would make the course more useful without making it longer?

## Interview prompts

Use short private conversations with a few completers and abandoners.

- Tell me where you first felt confident.
- Show me the point where you hesitated or stopped.
- What did you verify yourself after using AI?
- Which artifact would you show in a job interview?
- What would you change in the starter repository?
